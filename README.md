# National Unity and Prosperity Accord (NUPA)
### A Self-Funding, Sovereign Economic Operating System

## Executive Overview
The National Unity and Prosperity Accord (NUPA) is an open-source, private-sector economic operating system designed to solve the accelerating AI labor displacement crisis and discharge structural debt at **$0.00 taxpayer cost**. 

Rather than relying on fragile political promises, new taxes, or bureaucratic welfare programs, NUPA operates through **private contract law**, **existing property statutes**, and **99-year commercial ground leases**.

## The Core Problem
1. **The Automation Cliff:** As artificial intelligence and autonomous systems scale, corporations face a legal fiduciary duty (*Dodge v. Ford Motor Co.*) to cut labor costs and eliminate human payroll.
2. **The Failure of Politics:** Traditional legislative bills take years to pass, get watered down by lobbyists, and are easily dismantled by subsequent administrations.
3. **The Dependency Trap:** Universal Basic Income (UBI) leaves citizens dependent on a centralized state check that can be conditioned, inflated away, or revoked.

## The Structural Solution: Policy as Code

### 1. Sovereign Activation (Zero Taxpayer Risk)
Under existing federal statutory authority (**28 U.S.C. § 1505 / The Indian Tucker Act**), dormant, non-conservation public land managed by the Bureau of Land Management (capped at 10% / 24.5M acres total) is licensed to Sovereign Tribal Nations via a voluntary **Memorandum of Sovereign Understanding (MOSU)** . 
* The Host Tribe holds **absolute veto power** over all commercial leases and tenant selection.
* Zero tribal, sacred, or reservation land is encumbered.
* The remaining 90% of federal holdings (~220.5M acres) are permanently locked into untouchable conservation easements.

### 2. The Atomic Unit Mandate (Decentralized Hub Architecture)
Instead of creating a massive, centralized bureaucracy, development is strictly modular:
* **The 8,000-Acre Saturation Guardrail:** Development is subdivided into **3,000 independent, decentralized hubs capped at exactly 8,000 acres each** (with a cap of 5,000 verified beneficiary households per hub). 
* **Independent VQPV Governance:** Each individual hub is governed by its own localized, four-seat **Variable Quad-Partite Vetting (VQPV) Board**:
  1. **Tribal Representative:** Holds Sovereign Seniority and the absolute, unassailable veto over all leases and operations.
  2. **Beneficiary Representative:** Protects the equity and lineage dividend interests of local descendants.
  3. **Local Government Official:** Regulatory liaison (e.g., local council or sheriff) ensuring full compliance with OSHA, EPA, and local safety standards (holds no veto; can raise regulatory red flags).
  4. **Bonded Fiduciary Expert:** Independent financial specialist managing neutral escrow accounts and quarterly distributions.
* **Segmented Liability:** Because each hub is an autonomous Community Land Trust (CLT) with its own board, an administrative issue or breach in one hub never affects or stalls the neighboring hubs.

### 3. Fixed Cost Arbitrage (Protecting Human Labor)
To prevent mass automation, participating commercial tenants (solar/wind microgrids, hyperscale data infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and logistics) receive an asset-backed **70% discount on land and power overhead** compared to standard market rates.

### 4. The 95% Human Labor Firewall
To maintain the 70% overhead discount, corporate tenants must contractually preserve a **95% human workforce ratio** across all job tiers. 
* **The 300% Penalty:** If a corporation replaces human staff with automated systems below the threshold, the 70% discount is revoked, and rent immediately spikes by **300% back to market rates** after a 10-day notice. 
* This turns automation into a ruinous balance-sheet loss, making human labor mathematically more profitable than machines.

### 5. The 40/40/20 Recursive Reinvestment Waterfall
All ground lease revenue bypasses central government pooling into independent, neutral escrow, automatically distributing through a hard-coded model:
* **40% directly to the Host Tribal Nation** (restoring permanent economic autonomy and settling historical claims).
* **40% directly to verified descendants of the 1865 property debt** (settling historic restitution as an unseizable, non-dilutable private property right under probate law).
* **20% permanently ring-fenced on-site** for captive microgrids, physical infrastructure maintenance, and workforce education.
